The Star That Should Not Exist

Before the first god opened its eyes, before time learned to move, there was the Primordial Void. Nothing existed, and nothing was missing. Then, at the edge of creation, a star appeared where no star belonged. You stand before an ancient observatory. The star pulses above you. Somewhere inside the building, a bell rings despite having no clapper. What will you do?
