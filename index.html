A Forgotten Place

You find yourself in an old archive whose existence seems to have been forgotten by the world. Dust covers the shelves. Ancient manuscripts lie scattered across a stone table. Strange symbols mark the walls, and a sealed door stands at the far end of the chamber. You do not know who built this place, why it was abandoned, or why some records appear to have been concealed. Nothing tells you what you must do. For now, the choice is yours.
